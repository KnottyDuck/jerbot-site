Licence texts

The full text of every licence above ships inside the game build. The MIT, ISC and 0BSD licences are short and near-identical in effect: use it, change it, ship it, keep the copyright notice. The SIL Open Font License additionally requires that a modified font be renamed, which is why Rug Office Sans is not called Plex.

Not on this page

Build-time tools — TypeScript, Vite and their dependencies — are not listed. They shape the build; none of their code ships to you. If you want the complete tree including those, ask and I’ll send it.
