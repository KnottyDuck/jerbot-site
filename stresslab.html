Strategy Stress Lab

Write down your trading rules. Then watch them run through thousands of fictional markets — calm years, crashes, and everything in between — before you ever trust them.

How it works

  1. Write your rulebook.

    Entries, exits, position size, loss limits — in plain terms.

  2. Run the gauntlet.

    Your rules play out across many seeded, fictional markets built on the same market engine as Rug Pulled!.

  3. Read the report.

    Where your rules held, where they broke, and how deep the worst drawdown went.

What it is not

It does not use real market data, it does not predict anything, and passing it does not mean a strategy will make money. It's a way to find the weak spots in your own rules, safely.

Who it's for

people who want to test their discipline before risking anything, and educators and organizations looking for a safe practice tool. Interested in using Stress Lab with a group or a classroom? Write to hello@jerbot.dev.

Stress Lab runs today as a browser preview on an earlier version of the game's market engine. It's being updated to the current engine before any wider release.

Fictional markets only. Not financial advice.
