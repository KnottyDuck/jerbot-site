PubMint

Design a token for your community, club or business — then create it by signing one transaction from your own wallet. No code.

How it works

  1. 01

    Design it free.

    Name, symbol, image, supply, description. Practise as much as you like.

  2. 02

    Review before you sign.

    See exactly what will happen before anything is created.

  3. 03

    You hold the keys.

    PubMint never holds your wallet or your funds.

Who it’s for

Loyalty and membership programs, communities, small businesses, and anyone who wants to learn how tokens work with practice wallets first.

Status

PubMint runs today as a local development build: practice wallets, valueless test currency, a private test network. It is not on any public blockchain yet, and nothing on this page is an offer to sell tokens or an investment.
