PubMint

Design a token for your community, club or business — then create it by signing one transaction from your own wallet. No code.

How it works

  1. 01

    Design it free.

    Name, symbol, image, supply, description. Practice as much as you like.

  2. 02

    Review before you sign.

    See exactly what will happen before anything is created.

  3. 03

    You hold the keys.

    PubMint never holds your wallet or your funds.

Features

Working in today's local build

  • Token builder

    Name, symbol, image, supply and description, with a live preview.

  • Cost review before you sign

    See every step and fee before anything is created. (Costs are simulated in the practice build.)

  • One signature from your wallet

    Your wallet creates and holds the token; PubMint never holds your keys or funds.

  • Modern token standard

    Built on Solana's Token-2022 program, with editable metadata.

  • Mint and manage

    Mint more supply and update details from a creator dashboard.

  • Practice wallets

    Practice the whole flow with valueless test currency, including practice purchases and refunds.

  • Discovery

    Browse tokens other creators have made on the practice network.

  • Secure sign-in

    One-time signed login from your wallet; no password.

Planned

  • Public test-network (devnet) runs, then a hosted service.

  • Plans for ongoing token management.

  • Mobile apps (iPhone first).

Today's build runs on a private practice network. Features may change before release.

Who it’s for

Loyalty and membership programs, communities, small businesses, and anyone who wants to learn how tokens work with practice wallets first.

Status

PubMint runs today as a local development build: practice wallets, valueless test currency, a private test network. It is not on any public blockchain yet, and nothing on this page is an offer to sell tokens or an investment.
