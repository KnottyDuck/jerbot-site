About Jerbot

Small on purpose.

I'm one person in Texas. Everything on this site was built by me and a handful of AI agents running on ordinary hardware — no team, no funding, no warehouse of GPUs.

That's not a constraint I'm apologizing for. It's the point. Software has gotten expensive to make for reasons that have nothing to do with the software. I'm finding out how far one person with the right tools actually gets, and the answer so far is: further than it should be.

Next is bringing those tools in-house — my own agents, on my own hardware, cheap enough to run indefinitely. Rented intelligence is a bill. Owned intelligence is leverage.

The games are how I show my work.
